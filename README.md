## 🎯 The challenge

Engineer-to-order (ETO) companies design and build products to each customer's specific requirements, so every order involves new engineering work. This makes product design knowledge-intensive and time-consuming, and much of the valuable knowledge from earlier projects is hard to find and reuse.

## 💡 Our approach

RECODE develops **deep learning methods** and **advanced recommendation systems** that support engineers in reaching **optimal product design** decisions in ETO companies.

**Research themes**
- 🏭 Engineer-to-order manufacturing and product design
- 🧠 Deep learning methods for engineering data
- 🎯 Recommendation systems for design decision support
- 📐 Optimal product design
